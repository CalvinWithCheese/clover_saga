The wobblebird wept in a teacup of rain,
While the moon wore pajamas and boarded a train.
A pickle named Plimble recited the sea,
Then sneezed into Tuesday and turned into three.

The socks in the chimney were humming in blue,
The clocks all forgot what a minute should do.
So sad little sadface, with marshmallow knees,
Wrote crumbs to the thunder and mailed them to cheese.
